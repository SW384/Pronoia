About Pronoia

What is Pronoia?

Pronoia is a medication and health-tracking app designed specifically for older adults and their caregivers.

The idea behind Pronoia came from recognizing that medication management can become overwhelming for older adults, especially when multiple medications, appointments, symptoms, and daily routines need to be managed at the same time.

Our Goal

Instead of creating another complicated health-management system, we wanted to create something simple, accessible, and centered around the relationship between older adults and their caregivers.

Why the name "Pronoia"?

Pronoia represents the belief that the people around you are conspiring to do you good.

We wanted the name to represent the idea behind the app: creating a connected circle of care where older adults can maintain their independence while still having support from the people who care about them.

Care Should Feel Connected, Not Complicated

At the heart of Pronoia is a simple idea: managing everyday health should feel organized and supportive, not overwhelming.

Features

Everything You Need in One Place

Pronoia brings the tools older adults and caregivers need together in one simple, organized experience.

💊 Medication Tracking

Keep medication names, dosages, and schedules organized in one easy-to-access place.

📅 Appointment Tracker

Keep track of upcoming appointments and important dates so they are easier to remember.

😊 Mood Journal

Record how you are feeling over time and create a personal record of your daily experiences.

📝 Symptom Tracking

Record symptoms alongside other health information to keep a more organized personal health record.

🔄 Refill & Pill Tracker

Keep track of medication supplies and refill information to help make medication routines easier to manage.

🤝 Caregiver Connection

Connect with trusted caregivers and share selected information so everyone can stay informed.

🌱 Adherence Plant

Turn medication consistency into visual progress with a virtual plant that grows as you maintain your medication routine.

📊 Health Report

Bring important information together in an organized health report that can help you keep track of your personal health information.

Designed for Simplicity

Pronoia is designed to make everyday health management feel less complicated, with clear information and tools that are easy to find and understand.

Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

Pronoia is designed to help older adults and caregivers organise medication and health information. We understand that health information is personal, and we take privacy very seriously.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Pronoia, the app may collect information such as account information, medication information, appointment information, mood entries, symptom information, and information shared through caregiver connections.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and improve Pronoia's features, help users organize their health information, and support connections between users and their chosen caregivers.

Information Sharing

Pronoia does not share information with a caregiver unless the user chooses to connect with that caregiver and share information through the app.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Children's Privacy

Pronoia is designed primarily for adults and their caregivers and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
