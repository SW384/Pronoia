About Pronoia

What is Pronoia?

Pronoia is a medication and health-tracking app designed specifically for older adults and their caregivers.

The idea behind Pronoia came from recognizing that medication management can become overwhelming for older adults, especially when multiple medications, appointments, symptoms, and daily routines need to be managed at the same time.

Instead of creating another complicated health-management system, we wanted to create something simple, accessible, and centered around the relationship between older adults and their caregivers.

Why the name "Pronoia"?

Pronoia represents the belief that the people around you are conspiring to do you good.

We wanted the name to represent the idea behind the app: creating a connected circle of care where older adults can maintain their independence while still having support from the people who care about them.

Features

Pronoia brings the tools older adults and caregivers need together in one simple, organized experience.

Medication Tracking

Keep medication information organized by recording medications, dosages, and schedules in one place.

Appointment Tracker

Keep track of upcoming appointments and important dates without relying entirely on memory.

Mood Journal

Record how you are feeling over time and create a personal record that can help users recognize patterns in their daily experiences.

Symptom Tracking

Document symptoms alongside other health information to help users keep a more organized personal record.

Refill & Pill Tracker

Keep track of medication supplies and refill information to help users stay organized.

Caregiver Connection

Connect seniors and caregivers so caregivers can stay informed about the information shared with them.

Adherence Plant 🌱

Pronoia turns medication consistency into visual progress through a virtual plant that grows as users maintain their medication routine.

Health Report

Organize information from the app into a health report that can help you keep your personal health information together.

Common Questions

How do I create an account?

Download Pronoia, select the appropriate account type, and follow the registration instructions.

How do I connect with a caregiver/older adult?

Use the connection feature within the app and enter the required account information.

How do I delete my account?

Open your account settings and select the account deletion option.

Is Pronoia medical advice?

No. Pronoia is a medication organization and health-tracking tool and does not replace professional medical advice.

Who is Pronoia for?

Pronoia is designed primarily for older adults and their caregivers.
